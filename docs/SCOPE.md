# Scope

This document separates the production spine from intentionally excluded or deferred features.

## Core — build as production systems

### Taxi and travel
- 3D taxi cockpit.
- Automatic route/travel system.
- Route choice and authored diversion/risk decisions.
- Light vehicle dynamics for physical believability.
- Passenger pickup, ride, drop-off, payment, consequences.
- Checkpoints and road events as authored/systemic route events.
- Taxi upgrades that affect information, comfort, legality, work, or risk.

### Social stealth
- Cover identity.
- Persistent claims/lies and contradiction detection.
- Passenger suspicion.
- Longer-term city/authority attention.
- Knowledge/fact acquisition and use.
- Cultural/local knowledge as progression.

### Passengers and narrative
- Routine, recurring, and story passengers.
- Authored branching dialogue.
- Persistent passenger memory.
- Relationships and romance where authored.
- Passenger attitudes toward humans kept distinct from affection/trust.
- Consequence events and future callbacks.

### Language
- Translator device/runtime.
- Language, dialect, slang, professional, and illegal translator packs.
- Partial understanding where appropriate.
- Pack availability, legality, quality, and risk as data.

### Radio and information
- Multiple stations.
- Music/talk/news/traffic/underground broadcasts as authored content.
- Broadcasts can expose usable world information.
- Passenger reactions to radio where authored.

### Economy and work
- Fares and expenses.
- Legal/official opportunities where identity permits them.
- Underground/illegal job network.
- Risky upgrades and services.
- Repairs/maintenance at a scope appropriate to narrative gameplay.

### Persistent spaces
- 3D garage.
- 3D home/small apartment or room.
- Physical or diegetic interaction with upgrades, possessions, messages, souvenirs, and identity-related objects.
- No base-building progression requirement.

### Presentation
- 3D world presentation around routes.
- Modular route scenery rather than open world.
- Important 2D puppet passengers integrated with 3D lighting/presentation.
- Weather/time/lighting only to the depth needed for atmosphere and gameplay.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Free/manual driving.
- Full vehicle simulation.
- Open-world city traversal.
- Large-scale traffic simulation.
- GTA-style police chases.
- Combat.
- Procedural runtime dialogue as the primary narrative model.
- Base building.
- Huge procedural crowds.
- Mandatory third-person player mode.
- Drone gameplay.

## Deferred unless justified later

- Large smart-glasses progression tree.
- Deep car tuning.
- Broad on-foot exploration outside home/garage and tightly authored spaces.
- Many species-specific simulation systems.
- Extensive procedural city generation.
- Advanced multiplayer/networking.
- Modding SDK.
- Large-scale character-generation factory as a blocker for game development.

Deferred means the architecture should not unnecessarily forbid the feature, but current work must not depend on it.
